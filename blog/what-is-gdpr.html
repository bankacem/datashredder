What is GDPR and How Does it Protect You?

Published: January 15, 2024

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is one of the toughest and most comprehensive privacy and security laws in the world. Enacted by the European Union (EU), it imposes strict obligations on organizations anywhere, so long as they target or collect data related to people in the EU.

Key Rights Granted Under GDPR

  • Right to Be Informed: Organizations must be completely transparent about how they collect, store, and process your data.
  • Right of Access: You can request a full copy of all personal data an organization has about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Under Article 17, you can request that an organization delete all of your personal data without undue delay.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can block or suppress the processing of your personal data.

How to Exercise Your Right to Erasure

By using tools like DataShredder, you can quickly generate legally-compliant erasure requests and send them directly to Data Protection Officers (DPO). Organizations have 30 days to respond and comply with your request, or explain the legitimate legal grounds on which they must retain it.
