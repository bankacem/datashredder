Understanding Your Data Privacy Rights

Published: January 15, 2024

Over the last decade, privacy rights have evolved significantly. Multiple regions have introduced powerful laws modeled after the EU's GDPR. Here is a brief look at some of the key frameworks around the world.

1. GDPR (European Union)

The standard-bearer for modern privacy. Applies to any company in the world that handles the personal data of individuals residing inside the European Economic Area (EEA).

2. CCPA / CPRA (California, USA)

Grants California residents robust consumer rights, including the right to know what personal data is being collected, whether it is sold or shared, and the right to request its deletion.

3. PDPL (Kingdom of Saudi Arabia)

The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) provides a comprehensive framework to protect individuals' privacy rights within Saudi Arabia, emphasizing data controller obligations, consent, and user access/deletion rights.

4. LGPD (Brazil)

The Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) unifies multiple separate privacy laws in Brazil, giving individuals explicit rights regarding access, deletion, correction, and transfer of personal details.
