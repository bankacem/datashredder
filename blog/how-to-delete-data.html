How to Delete Your Data from Major Platforms

Published: January 15, 2024

Major platforms like Google, Facebook, Amazon, and TikTok store massive amounts of information about your preferences, browsing history, geographic location, and offline habits. Luckily, global regulations mandate that they must delete this data upon request.

Step-by-Step Guide to Request Deletion

  1. Find the correct contact point: Most companies list their privacy contacts or Data Protection Officer (DPO) in their Privacy Policy footer. Look for email addresses like privacy@company.com or dpo@company.com.
  2. Generate a formal request: Draft a request specifying the legal framework (e.g. GDPR, CCPA). Our free tool on the homepage does this automatically.
  3. Submit and verify: Send the email from the email address linked to your account. Some companies may require additional verification or code confirmation.
  4. Wait for compliance: Companies generally have up to 30 days under GDPR and 45 days under CCPA to fulfill your request and confirm.

What if They Refuse?

Unless there is a strong legal reason (such as tax/audit laws, open fraud investigations, or active financial transactions), they are obligated to comply. If they refuse unfairly, you can escalate the matter to your local data protection authority.
