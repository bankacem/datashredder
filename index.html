❓ Frequently Asked Questions


            

                

                    

                        
What is the Right to be Forgotten?

                        +
                    

                    

                        
The Right to be Forgotten (also known as Right to Erasure) is a provision under GDPR Article 17 that allows individuals to request deletion of their personal data from companies. Similar rights exist under CCPA, LGPD, and other privacy laws.

                    

                


                

                    

                        
Is this service really free?

                        +
                    

                    

                        
Yes! The basic deletion request generator is 100% free with no limits. We offer an optional Pro plan ($5/month) with tracking features, but the core tool will always be free.

                    

                


                

                    

                        
Do you store my personal information?

                        +
                    

                    

                        
No! Everything happens in your browser. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information you enter. Your privacy is our priority.

                    

                


                

                    

                        
How long do companies have to respond?

                        +
                    

                    

                        
Under GDPR: 30 days (extendable to 60 in complex cases). Under CCPA: 45 days (extendable to 90). Most companies respond faster to avoid penalties.

                    

                


                

                    

                        
What if the company refuses my request?

                        +
                    

                    

                        
Companies must provide a valid legal reason for refusal (e.g., legal obligation to keep data). If refused unfairly, you can file a complaint with your data protection authority or seek legal advice.

                    

                


                

                    

                        
Can I use this for any company?

Yes! Works for Facebook, Google, Amazon, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, banks, retailers, or any organization that has your personal data.
