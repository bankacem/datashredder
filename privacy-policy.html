Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2024

At DataShredder, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle any information when you use our website and generator tool.

1. No Data Storage or Transmission

We do not store, save, or transmit any of the details you enter into our generator form (including your full name, email address, company name, or privacy emails). Everything is processed entirely in your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.

2. Client-Side Processing

When you click "Generate Deletion Request", our code compiles the text dynamically in memory and presents it directly to you. Your inputs never reach our servers, keeping your identity and intentions completely private.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not track you or drop any advertising/tracking cookies by default. We may use simple, privacy-focused analytics to monitor page traffic without tracking individual users or IP addresses.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party services and government privacy authorities. We have no control over their privacy practices, and recommend reading their policies when visiting them.
